Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE] · Last updated: [DATE]

The short version

  • Your health information stays on your device. Your symptom logs, food notes, challenge results and settings are saved only in your web browser on the device you use. They are never sent to us.
  • We have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.
  • We don't sell or share your information, because we never receive it.
  • A few outside services are involved in running the app, such as the website host. They receive basic technical information like your IP address. These are listed below.

2. Information that stays on your device

When you use the app, it saves the following in your browser's local storage on your device:

  • Your daily logs: symptom scores (bloating, pain, gas, urgency, stress), stool type, what you ate, and notes
  • Your program progress: phase, start dates, reintroduction challenges and results, checklist answers, and any "jump in" details you enter (such as estimated symptom levels and notes about your situation)
  • App settings, badges and reminders (for example, when you last made a backup)
  • Saved results of food look-ups (ingredient summaries from Open Food Facts and Wikipedia), kept for 7 days

The app also stores a copy of its own files on your device (a "service worker" cache) so it can work offline. This cache contains the app, not your information.

We cannot see, access or recover this information. It's deleted if you tap Start over in Settings, clear your browser's data for this site, or uninstall the app. Some browsers may also delete website data on their own; for example, Safari can delete data from websites you haven't used for 7 days unless the app is added to your home screen.

3. Backups you create

If you choose Save a backup file, the app creates a file containing your information on your device. You decide where it goes. If you save it to a cloud service such as iCloud Drive or Google Drive, or email it, that service's own privacy policy applies. We never receive your backup.

5. Health information

Information about your digestive symptoms and diet is health information. Many privacy laws treat it as especially sensitive. We designed the app so that this information never leaves your device unless you choose to send it (for example, by saving a backup or using an optional Claude feature).

IBS Domination is a consumer app. It is not offered by or on behalf of a doctor, hospital or health insurer, so the U.S. health privacy law HIPAA does not generally apply to it. That's also why we chose to keep your information on your device.

Washington State residents: see our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

6. Cookies and tracking

The app does not use cookies, advertising, analytics, tracking pixels or third-party trackers. It uses browser local storage only to save your information on your device, as described above.

7. Children

The app is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The strict low FODMAP diet is generally not advised for children without the guidance of their clinician. If you are a parent and have questions, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

8. Your choices and rights

  • See your information: it's all in the app (Log, Quest and Today), and you can export it as a CSV file or backup file at any time.
  • Correct it: edit any log in the Log tab.
  • Delete it: use Start over in Settings, delete individual logs, or clear your browser's data for this site.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights over your personal information, for example under the EU or UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA, or California's CCPA. Because we don't receive your app information, most requests can be met directly in the app. For anything else, contact [CONTACT EMAIL].

9. Security

Your information is protected by your device and browser. We recommend using a device passcode and keeping your browser up to date. The app is served over HTTPS.
