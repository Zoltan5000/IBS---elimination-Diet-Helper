Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE] · Last updated: [DATE]

This policy explains how IBS Domination, operated by [OWNER LEGAL NAME], handles "consumer health data" as defined by Washington's My Health My Data Act (House Bill 1155).

Categories of consumer health data

The app lets you record information about your digestive health: symptom scores, stool type, foods eaten, notes, reintroduction challenge results and estimated symptom levels. This information is stored only on your device. We do not collect, receive, access or store it.

Sources and purpose

The information comes only from what you enter. The app uses it on your device to show your progress, charts, food checks and suggestions.

Sharing

We don't share or sell consumer health data. The app sends limited information to outside services only when you use certain features:

  • If you use Search the web in Meal ideas, the search shown on screen opens on the search engine you choose (Google, DuckDuckGo or Bing). It can include foods you chose to leave out, which may reflect your trigger foods.
  • If you tap Look up on a food or dish, or check a food the app doesn't recognise, the food's name is sent to Open Food Facts and Wikipedia to find its usual ingredients. It can hint at what you eat. Nothing else is sent.
  • If you open the app on claude.ai and choose an "Ask Claude" option, the menu or meal request you chose, plus a summary of your reintroduction results, is sent to Anthropic through your own Claude account.

The website host (GitHub Pages) logs your IP address when you open the app, as described in our Privacy Policy.

Your rights

You can view, export, correct and delete your information at any time in the app (Log tab, and Settings → Start over).
